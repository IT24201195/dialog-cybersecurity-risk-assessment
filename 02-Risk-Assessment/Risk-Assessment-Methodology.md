
### Likelihood

| Score | Rating | Meaning |
|------:|--------|---------|
| 1 | Rare | Very unlikely |
| 2 | Unlikely | Could occur, but not expected |
| 3 | Possible | Could realistically occur |
| 4 | Likely | Expected to occur periodically |
| 5 | Almost Certain | Highly likely/frequent |

### Impact

| Score | Rating | Meaning |
|------:|--------|---------|
| 1 | Negligible | Minimal business impact |
| 2 | Minor | Limited disruption |
| 3 | Moderate | Noticeable operational/financial impact |
| 4 | Major | Significant business/customer impact |
| 5 | Severe | Major financial, regulatory, operational or reputational consequences |

### Risk Score

Likelihood × Impact
### Risk Level

| Score | Risk Level |
|-------|------------|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |
