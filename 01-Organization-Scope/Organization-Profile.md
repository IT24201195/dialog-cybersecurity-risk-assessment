# Dialog Axiata PLC - Organization Profile

## 1. Organization Overview
Dialog Axiata PLC is Sri Lanka's largest integrated telecommunications and digital services provider. The company offers mobile, fixed broadband, television, enterprise technology solutions and digital financial services to millions of customers across Sri Lanka.

As a critical telecommunications operator, Dialog manages extensive digital infrastructure supporting consumer, enterprise and government services. Its operations rely on continuous availability, protection of customer information, secure payment processing and resilient network infrastructure.

This assessment is based entirely on publicly available information and does not represent an internal security audit.

## 8. Assessment Scope
This assessment evaluates cybersecurity risks affecting publicly identifiable business operations, information assets and technology environments associated with Dialog Axiata PLC.
